FAQ

Questions

Is the GR3 legal for US karting?

The GR3 carries Snell SA2020 certification and FIA 8859-2015 homologation — the two standards US karting and auto racing series most commonly accept at tech inspection. Series rulebooks vary, so check yours; both listings are public (fia.com / smf.org) for your tech inspector to verify.

Why does factory assembly matter for a custom helmet?

A helmet is certified as a complete product. If a third party disassembles and repaints it, the certification chain breaks. In our program the factory paints the shell and completes final assembly, so your custom helmet arrives as the same certified product that was homologated.

How long does a custom helmet take?

Typically 4–6 weeks from design approval to US delivery. Before dispatch, we send you photos of your finished helmet for approval. Stock helmets ship much faster when US stock is available.

Can I exchange it if the fit is wrong?

Stock helmets can be exchanged for size within 30 days if unworn on track. Custom-painted helmets are made to your design and can't be resold, so we confirm your size carefully with you before painting begins.
